Play your way

  • Practice against three computer players at any time. No account and no internet needed.
  • Play with friends on the same Wi-Fi: one device hosts, the others join in seconds. Bots fill empty seats.
  • Play online with Game Center (2–4 players): invite friends or quick-match.
  • Push-to-talk voice messages in Wi-Fi games and in games with invited Game Center friends.

Real rules, made easy

  • Full Guobiao scoring: all 81 fans, flower tiles and a minimum-fan setting (0–16).
  • Winning-path hints show which tiles to keep, what you wait for and how many fan you can reach.
  • A fan table with example tiles for every fan, and a result sheet that shows the tiles behind each fan.
  • Game Center achievements and leaderboards.

Built for every screen

  • Perspective or top view.
  • iPhone in landscape or portrait, iPad and Mac.
  • English and Simplified Chinese.

规则完整，上手简单

  • 番种表附示例牌，结算单显示每个番种对应的牌。
