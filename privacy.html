Mahjong Mayhem — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Mahjong Mayhem ("the app") is a mahjong game for iPhone, iPad and Mac. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app. In short: we do not collect any data about you.

No data collection

  • The app has no accounts, no sign-up and no login of its own.
  • The app contains no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not use third-party SDKs.
  • The developer does not run any server for the app and receives no information from it.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your settings on your device only: the player name and avatar you pick, language, sound and music settings, and table options. This information never leaves your device except as described below for multiplayer games. Deleting the app deletes this information.

Local network play

When you host or join a game on the same Wi-Fi network, the app uses your local network to find nearby tables and connects directly to the other devices (peer to peer). The game data (moves, your chosen player name and avatar) goes only to the other players at your table. It does not go to the developer or to any server.

Game Center play

If you choose to play online through Apple Game Center, Apple handles sign-in and matchmaking under Apple's privacy policy. Game data goes between the players in the match. Voice messages are only available in games with friends you invited; random quick-match games have no voice messages. The developer receives no personal information from Game Center.

Microphone and voice messages

Voice chat in the app is push-to-talk: you hold the microphone button to record a short voice message.

  • Voice messages are available only at a local Wi-Fi table and in Game Center games with friends you invited. Random quick-match games and practice games against computer players never use the microphone.
  • The app uses the microphone only while you hold the button. The app asks for microphone access the first time you use it.
  • The voice message is sent directly to the other players at your table and played on their devices. It is not stored on any server and not sent to the developer.
  • The voice clip is deleted after it is sent and played.
  • You can turn off microphone access at any time in your device settings.

Fan Calculator web page

The Fan Calculator on this website runs entirely in your browser. A photo you take or choose there is shown only on your device as a reference. It is never uploaded or stored, and the page sends no data anywhere.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

麻将狂欢 — 隐私政策

最后更新：2026年11月8日

“麻将狂欢”（以下简称“本应用”）是一款适用于 iPhone、iPad 和 Mac 的麻将游戏。本政策说明你在使用本应用时信息如何处理。简而言之：我们不收集任何关于你的数据。

不收集数据

  • 本应用没有账号，无需注册或登录。
  • 本应用不含统计分析、广告或跟踪功能，也不使用任何第三方 SDK。
  • 开发者不为本应用运营任何服务器，也不会从本应用接收任何信息。

保存在你设备上的信息

本应用只在你的设备上保存设置：你选择的玩家名字和头像、语言、音效和音乐设置以及牌桌选项。除下文所述的多人游戏外，这些信息不会离开你的设备。删除本应用即会删除这些信息。

局域网对战

当你在同一 Wi-Fi 网络中创建或加入房间时，本应用使用局域网查找附近的牌桌，并与其他设备直接连接（点对点）。游戏数据（出牌操作、你选择的名字和头像）只会发送给同桌的其他玩家，不会发送给开发者或任何服务器。

Game Center 联机

如果你选择通过 Apple Game Center 联机，登录和匹配由 Apple 依照 Apple 隐私政策 处理。游戏数据只在同场玩家之间传输。只有与你邀请的好友对战时才能发送语音消息，随机匹配的对局没有语音。开发者不会从 Game Center 获得任何个人信息。

麦克风与语音消息

本应用的语音聊天为“按住说话”：按住麦克风按钮即可录制一条简短的语音消息。

  • 只有在局域网牌桌和与受邀好友的 Game Center 对局中才能使用语音消息。随机匹配的对局和与电脑对战的单机练习从不使用麦克风。
  • 本应用只在你按住按钮时使用麦克风。首次使用时，本应用会请求麦克风权限。
  • 语音消息直接发送给同桌的其他玩家，并在他们的设备上播放，不会保存在任何服务器上，也不会发送给开发者。
  • 语音片段在发送和播放后即被删除。
  • 你可以随时在设备设置中关闭麦克风权限。

算番器网页

本网站的算番器完全在你的浏览器中运行。你在页面上拍摄或选择的照片只在你的设备上显示供对照，不会上传或保存，页面也不会发送任何数据。

儿童

本应用不会有意收集任何人（包括儿童）的个人信息。

政策变更

如本政策有变更，我们会更新本页面及上方日期。
